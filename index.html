GlobalMart: Supply Chain & Business Performance

Question: How profitable is GlobalMart, and how well is its supply chain performing?

What I did: Cleaned the raw dataset, calculated the KPIs in Excel and cross-checked them in Power BI, then designed a two-page Power BI dashboard covering profitability and supply chain operations.

Key findings

  • Revenue of $703.5M and profit of $195.5M across 2,010 orders, a 27.79% profit margin.
  • Only 34.38% of deliveries arrived on time; 65.07% were late.
  • Damaged goods stayed low at 2.49%, so delivery speed is the bigger problem.

ShopTrack: Sales & Operations Dashboard

Question: Where does revenue come from, and how reliable is delivery?

What I did: Cleaned a messy sales dataset and built an interactive dashboard with a region slicer, revenue trends, top customers, and delivery status.

Key findings

  • Total revenue of $739.3M from 601 orders (6,215 units).
  • West was the top region at $172.9M; Laptop was the top category at $214.8M.
  • 52% of orders were delivered late.
June 2026Power BI · Three-page dashboard

Finance Job Market & Recruitment Intelligence

Question: What do finance employers hire for, and what skills and salaries do they ask for?

What I did: Analysed finance job postings and built a three-page Power BI dashboard covering the job market, salary and skills demand, and recruitment efficiency.

Key findings

  • 2,312 postings from about 2,000 hiring companies, with 468K applicants.
  • Hiring is split almost evenly between full-time, contract, and internship roles.
  • Audit and corporate finance were the most active hiring departments; Data Analyst was the most common job title.
May 2026Excel · Pivot Tables · Slicers · Dashboard

HR Workforce Analytics Dashboard

Question: What does the workforce look like by department, business unit, gender, and age?

What I did: Built three interactive Excel dashboard pages (workforce overview, training and HR investment, employee performance and engagement) with pivot tables, slicers, and KPI cards.

Key findings

  • 2,845 employees with an average tenure of 5 years (1,588 female, 1,257 male).
  • Production is the largest department with 1,910 employees.
  • The 56+ age group is the largest, with 1,178 employees.

These dashboards were built from practice and sample datasets for training projects.
